Pro bono legal aid clinic

Report a legal problem

Facing a legal problem as a FUTO student? Tell us about it. The FBA Legal Aid Clinic will assign you a student barrister, free of charge, to advise you and help you take the next step.

How the clinic works

Every report is read by the clinic's administrators and matched with a student barrister of the association.

  1. 1. Tell us what happened

    Fill in the form below with your details and a clear account of the problem. You will get a reference number straight away.

  2. 2. We assign a barrister

    An administrator reviews your report and assigns a student barrister suited to the matter as soon as possible.

  3. 3. Follow your case

    Use your reference and matric number to check the status of your case and see who your barrister is and how to reach them.

Please note. The clinic is run by law students of the FUTO Bar Association. It offers free guidance, help with letters, petitions and internal university processes, and referral to qualified lawyers where needed. It is not a substitute for a licensed legal practitioner. If you are in immediate danger, contact the police or campus security first.

Submit a case

Open to FUTO students. Your report is confidential: only clinic administrators and the barrister assigned to you can see it.

Cases that are resolved may later appear in our case archive, but only as anonymised summaries with every name and identifying detail removed.

About you

Your 11-digit FUTO registration number, e.g. 20201234567.

Your problem

For example: “Withheld result after fee payment”.

Include dates, who was involved, what you have already done and what outcome you want. Don't upload documents yet; your barrister will ask for them.

Case received

Your reference number

Write this down or take a screenshot. You will need it, together with your matric number, to check the progress of your case below. An administrator will assign a barrister as soon as possible.

Check the status of your case

Enter the reference number you were given and the matric number you submitted with.
