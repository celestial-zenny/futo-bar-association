About the association

Mission and purpose

The FUTO Bar Association is the body of intending barristers at the Federal University of Technology Owerri. It sits under the Judiciary of the Students' Union Government and is governed by its own written constitution.

Our mission

The members of the FBA formed the association, in the words of its preamble, to establish a just and humane society, promote the common good of the association, establish justice, ensure domestic tranquillity, conserve and develop our patrimony, and ensure law and order — securing the blessings of liberty for the members of the association and for those who come after them.

Why the FBA exists

The association's stated objective is to preserve, protect and defend the rule of law in line with the Human Rights Declaration of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and the constitution of the Students' Union Government, among the students of FUTO. In practice that means three things: a forum where legal thinking is taught, a voice that speaks up when students are treated unfairly, and a standard of professional conduct its members are held to.

What membership offers

Members train through the SUG School of Paralegal Studies (SSPS), whose Level 1 I-Barrister classes cover judicial analysis, jurisprudence and constitutional law, professional ethics, the legislative process, advocacy and leadership. Weekly notes and lecturers' hand-outs are published on this site for every member to download.

Read more about the association and its aims.

  • Justice

    To condemn and oppose all forms of injustice, oppression and victimisation through constitutional means.

  • Equity

    To encourage and maintain a cordial, peaceful and respectful relationship among all members, irrespective of differences.

  • Fairness

    To create a veritable forum to promote legality, and to represent the students of FUTO fairly and actively.

  • Legal literacy

    To promote and develop the physical, social, mental and intellectual prowess of members through structured study.

Leadership

Executive Council

The Executive Council carries out the day-to-day running of the association and is accountable to its entire membership. Officers are elected and take the Oath of Office before assuming their duties.

  • President

    Photograph to be added

    Head of the association and of the Executive Council. Presides over all executive and general meetings.

  • Vice President

    Photograph to be added

    Assists the President, acts in their absence, and holds the seal of the association.

  • Secretary General

    Photograph to be added

    Attorney-General of the association in all legal matters. Convenes meetings and keeps its records and correspondence.

  • Treasurer

    Photograph to be added

    Keeps the accounts of the association and is a signatory to its funds, in line with the finance article of the constitution.

  • Public Relations Officer

    Photograph to be added

    Speaks for the association, publicises its activities, and keeps members informed of sittings and programmes.

Officer names and photographs for the current session will be published here once the Executive Council confirms them. See the full duties of each office.
