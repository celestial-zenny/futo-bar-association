Level 1 course outline

I-Barrister Classes (Advocacy & Foundation). The stated goal: master the basics of law, student rights and campus political entry.

The six courses of SSPS Level 1 and their topics, as published in the SSPS courses outline.
Course Topics
JCY 111 — Judicial Analysis & Litigation Etymology and ranks of the Judiciary; roles of court officers and the court clerk; steps in civil and criminal litigation; procedures for filing a case; legal precedents and Latin maxims; symbolic representation of Mother Themis.
LAW 112 — Jurisprudence & Constitutional Law Concept, types and purpose of jurisprudence; types and features of a Constitution; the Rule of Law and Separation of Powers; historical background of constitutionalism; application of the philosophy of law.
EPR 113 — Professional Ethics & Advocacy Canon of Ethics and Law vs. Ethics; lawyer and client relationship; basic communication skills and key elements; public speaking and advocacy techniques.
PTS 114 — Legislative Process & Time Management The Parliament and importance of law-making; types of Bills and processes for passing a bill; time management in a legal context; meeting procedures and administrative discipline.
ADV 115 — Advocacy, Activism & Petitioning Strategies for speaking up and taking action; architecture of a formal petition; evidence gathering and documentation of incidents; procedures for filing petitions to SUG and Management; practical case-filing workshop.
LDR 101 — Leadership 101 (Politics & Positioning) Analysis of FUTO SUG and political structure; strategies for political positioning and branding; aspirations and timing for leadership roles; political do's and don'ts and campus-related issues; leadership strategies and grassroots mobilisation.
