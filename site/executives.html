Leadership

Executive Council

All officers of the association constitute the Executive Council. It carries out the day-to-day running of the FBA in conformity with the constitution and is accountable to the entire membership.

The five principal offices

Officers are elected on the day of the election and may not begin to perform the functions of office until they have taken the Oath of Office and of Allegiance provided in the constitution.

Duties of each office

Summarised from Article 5 of the constitution of the association, which sets out the officers and their duties.

Duties of the principal officers of the FUTO Bar Association.
Office Duties under the constitution
President Head of the association and of the Executive Council. Acts as the association's official delegation, summons executive and general meetings through or with the Secretary General, and presides over them. Responsible for setting up committees and panels necessary for the betterment of the association, appointing their chairmen, and signing indicted barrister certificates alongside the Secretary General to affirm their membership. Forwards financial and other certified contenders of the Bench to the SRC in accordance with the constitution, and fills vacant positions by appointment after the application of Article 12.
Vice President Assists the President in their duties and acts in their absence. Where the office of President falls vacant, holds the seal and, in any case, the office of the President pending the conduct of a bye-election. Carries out all functions assigned by the constitution or by the President.
Secretary General Attorney-General of the association in all legal matters and its representative in court, or delegates that role to the Assistant Secretary General. Responsible for the general administrative duties of the association. Convenes an executive meeting in consultation with the President and a general meeting at the President's request or when the need arises. Keeps attendance books, minutes and records of both executive and general meetings, and receives, records and countersigns all external correspondence.
Treasurer Custodian of the funds and accounts of the association, keeping records of income and expenditure and acting as a signatory to association cheques together with the President and Financial Secretary, in line with the finance article of the constitution.
Public Relations Officer The public voice of the association. Publicises sittings, programmes and decisions, keeps members informed, and manages the association's relationship with the wider student body and the Students' Union Government.

The constitution also provides for an Assistant Secretary General, Financial Secretary, Public Relations Officers, a Director of Social and a Provost, all of whom form part of the Executive Council. Read the full text in the FBA Constitution (PDF), or see all governing documents.

Accountability

The constitution binds officers as tightly as it empowers them.

  • Code of Conduct Panel

    A panel of five members, chaired by a Senior Advocate of FUTO where one is available, investigates allegations of misconduct against any member — including members of the Executive Council — and prescribes punishment for indicted members.

  • Removal from office

    Article 7 provides the process for removing an officer. A member of the Code of Conduct Panel is removed only where a motion for removal is signed by at least ten financially up-to-date members and passed by two-thirds of the members at a properly constituted meeting.

  • Standards for executives

    An officer may not put themselves in a position where personal interest conflicts with their duties, nor take or accept property or take part in any society incompatible with the dignity of the office, nor abuse the office or take arbitrary actions prejudicial to members.
